# Samudra Manthana

Churning of the ocean during Dasara

## Themes

- Whatever we do, there is help from many others and many things
- Vishnu doesn't restrict to doing or being one thing
- He is favorable

## Relations

- Churning of the cosmos to produce heavy metals, organic compounds, planets
- Churning of Veda to produce knowledge
- Churning in the body to produce activity and outcomes
