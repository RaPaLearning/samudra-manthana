Here is the sequence of the Churning of the Ocean (Samudra Manthana) in short sentences:

* Sage Durvasa curses the Devas, causing them to lose their powers.
* The Asuras gain power and easily defeat the weakened Devas.
* Lord Vishnu suggests churning the cosmic ocean to obtain Amrita, the nectar of immortality.
* The Devas and Asuras form a temporary alliance to share the work and the nectar.
* They use Mount Mandara as the churning rod and the giant serpent Vasuki as the rope.
* Vishnu takes the Kurma (turtle) avatar to support the sinking mountain on his back.
* The Asuras hold the serpent's head, while the Devas hold its tail.
* The churning first releases a deadly, world-ending poison called Halahala.
* Lord Shiva drinks the poison to save the universe, holding it in his throat and turning it blue.
* The churning continues, and numerous divine treasures and beings emerge from the ocean.
* Goddess Lakshmi appears and chooses Vishnu as her eternal consort.
* Finally, the divine physician Dhanvantari emerges holding the pot of Amrita.
* The Asuras immediately seize the nectar and refuse to share it.
* Vishnu transforms into Mohini, a stunningly beautiful enchantress.
* Mohini distracts the Asuras and cleverly distributes the nectar only to the Devas.
* The Devas drink the nectar, regain their immortality, and defeat the Asuras in battle.
